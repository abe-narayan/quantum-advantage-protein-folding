# Candidate pool and deep-audit shortlist (synthesis of 12 discovery lenses)

Date: 2026-09-28. Status: DISCOVERY SYNTHESIS. These are hypotheses for audit, not results.
Inputs: `discovery_{algorithms,complexity,claims,amo,materials,chemistry,sciml_inference,optimization,classical_frontier,qsim_dynamics,alt_domains,resources}.md`.

Novelty categories: A no quantum paper; B quantum methods nearby, not this exact problem; C quantum methods exist for the problem, but no quantum-vs-classical advantage study; D advantage claimed only against weak classical methods; E claimed, then challenged or dequantized; F established.
All novelty statements are **scoped**: they cover arXiv abstract/listing search, Crossref and OpenAlex (partial) through 2026-09-28. WebSearch was exhausted and OpenAlex/S2 were rate-limited for most lenses, so journal-only work may be missing. "Not found" is not proof of absence.

## 1. Elimination filters applied

Each kill below cites at least one of these filters:

- **F-claimed**: the problem is already claimed or established (C with resource estimates, or F), and no materially distinct subproblem remains.
- **F-classical**: the strongest classical method already closes the gap, or a multi-family classical consensus exists.
- **F-quad**: the speedup is quadratic or low-polynomial and does not survive fault-tolerant overhead (Babbush et al. 2021).
- **F-load**: state preparation, the oracle, data loading or readout dominates the cost.
- **F-sim**: the problem is classically simulable in the relevant regime (high-T, short-time, 1D, small active space, sign-free).
- **F-toy**: toy model, contrived instance, or no practical user.
- **F-bench**: there is no benchmark path or no fair classical comparator.
- **L1-L7**: the protein-failure patterns. L1 weak baseline; L2 solver equivalence; L3 argmin or search over a classical landscape; L4 simulator runtime; L5 information vs computation; L6 model floor larger than solver error; L7 single-family hardness.

## 3. Shortlist for deep audit (6 distinct directions, at most 2 per domain)

Domain balance: HED plasma (C01), atomic astrophysics (C04), nuclear astrophysics (C07), 3D quantum magnetism (C14), AMO continuum dynamics (C41), semiconductor ultrafast optics (C44). AMO/atomic appears twice (C04 bound-bound thermal spectra, C41 continuum ionization dynamics); the mechanisms and users differ. Every candidate's quantum object is a real-time or spectral **quantum** correlator or a Born-rule sample of a many-body continuum state, never the argmin of a classical energy (L3). None of their speedups rest on Grover or amplitude estimation.

### S1 = C01. XRTS dynamic structure factor of degenerate warm dense matter
- **Problem.** INPUT: species (H/D, Be, CH), r_s 1-4, theta 0.25-1, XRTS wavevector q, ion snapshots. COMPUTE: the thermal correlator <rho_q(t) rho_-q(0)>_beta out to t ~ hbar/(instrument resolution). OUTPUT: S_ee(q,w) with error bars and the (rho, T, Z) posterior from measured NIF/LCLS spectra. Scaling: N_e 16 -> 256 at fixed (r_s, theta), and decreasing theta. Validation: on the UEG, the Laplace transform of the quantum S must reproduce exact PIMC F(q,tau).
- **Quantum mechanism.** First-quantized plane-wave electrons (Su 2021; Rubin 2024 non-BO machinery). The thermal state comes from microcanonical QPE filtering, TPQ or a detailed-balance Gibbs sampler. Readout by Hadamard test or linear-response pumping. The advantage is exponential over exact fermionic real-time dynamics.
- **Classical twin.** PIMC with xi-extrapolation and Taylor-xi; model-free ITCF analysis; MaxEnt/PyLIT continuation; LR-TDDFT/Liouville-Lanczos; mixed stochastic TDDFT; Chihara/average atom; G1-G2 NEGF; small-N ED.
- **Novelty (scoped B).** Quantum protocols exist for WDM stopping power, opacity and conductivity. The searches found no quantum S_ee(q,w) or XRTS study and no comparison against PIMC+ITCF.
- **Kill test first (classical, cheap).** Take exact small-N spectra and measure how much the real-frequency S adds to the (rho, T, Z) posterior beyond the ITCF at realistic instrument resolution. If the posterior width gain is below 2x, kill (L5).

### S2 = C41. Correlated multi-electron photo- and strong-field ionization
- **Problem.** INPUT: atom with eta active electrons (He validation; Li XUV triple photoionization; Ne/Ar non-sequential multiple ionization) and pulse parameters. COMPUTE: full 3·eta-D TDSE. OUTPUT: samples from the joint photoelectron momentum distribution; multiple-ionization yields and ratios; differential cross sections to compare with COLTRIMS/XFEL coincidence data. Scaling: eta, box size, and wavelength.
- **Quantum mechanism.** First-quantized grid simulation with 3·eta·log2 N qubits, coherent Coulomb arithmetic, and QFT-to-momentum readout, which gives Born-rule samples of the continuum wavefunction. Memory is exponential classically, gate cost is polynomial on the quantum side. Claim target: category 3 (resource) plus category 4 (sampling), the latter verifiable through low-order marginals.
- **Classical twin.** TDCC/HELIUM (exact for eta=2); RMT; TD-CASSCF; TD-ORMAS/TD-RASCI; MCTDHF; tSURFF; TDDFT; CTMC.
- **Kill test first.** Find a measured channel where converged RAS/MCTDHF/RMT results disagree with each other or with experiment. If truncated-correlation methods converge for eta=3, kill.

### S3 = C14. Finite-T S(q,w) of 3D dipolar-octupolar pi-flux quantum spin ice
- **Problem.** INPUT: XYZ(+Jxz) parameter sets for Ce pyrochlores, g-tensor, T, and N = 16L^3 (128/432 spins). COMPUTE: finite-T polarized correlators. OUTPUT: SF/NSF S(q,w), spinon continuum, low-energy weight, and a decision version (a few (q,w) points, 2-5 hypotheses, 0-flux vs pi-flux) scored against Gao et al. polarized INS data. Validation rung: sign-free 0-flux QMC+SAC.
- **Quantum mechanism.** Trotter or qubitization of a local 3D spin Hamiltonian, finite-T state preparation, Hadamard-test or linear-response readout. The advantage rests on real-time entanglement in 3D combined with the QMC sign problem.
- **Classical twin.** 32-site ED; GMFT; SCEBR; parton/Schwinger bosons; semiclassical MD; NLCE; 3D BP-TN; t-VMC/NQS dynamics; sparse Pauli dynamics at high T.
- **Kill test first.** Do the existing approximate methods (GMFT, SCEBR, ED32, MD) disagree on the likelihood ranking at INS resolution? If they agree, there is no decision flip.

### S4 = C07. Hot neutron-rich matter spin/density response for supernova neutrino opacities
- **Problem.** INPUT: lattice pionless/LO chiral EFT fitted to phase shifts; n = 0.01-0.3 n0; T 3-15 MeV; Y_p 0-0.3; q. COMPUTE: finite-T real-time spin-density and density correlators. OUTPUT: S_A(q,w), S_V(q,w) and opacity corrections relative to RPA/virial tables. Scaling: lattice volume and Y_p. Validation: unitary Fermi gas S(q,w) against sign-free AFQMC and Bragg data.
- **Quantum mechanism.** Lattice-EFT Hamiltonian simulation (Watson 2312.05344), thermal-state preparation, linear-response / Gaussian-integral-transform spectral estimation. The advantage is exponential where tensor/spin-orbit/3N forces or Y_p>0 bring back the sign problem.
- **Classical twin.** Virial expansion; RPA/Landau; lattice-EFT pinhole traces; AFQMC + inversion; CC-GIT (T=0); AFDMC; NQS.
- **Kill test first.** Two checks. Is the relevant low-density, low-Y_p window sign-free (SU(4)-like LO), in which case AFQMC closes the gap? And do opacities depend on more than the static structure factor and low moments (L5)?

### S5 = C04. Kilonova lanthanide/actinide expansion opacity from thermal dipole spectral functions
- **Problem.** INPUT: ion (Nd II, Er III, U II, ...), relativistic active-space Hamiltonian (60-120 spinors), T 3000-10000 K, wavelength bins 0.3-3 um. COMPUTE: the thermal dipole spectral function binned in wavelength, together with the line-strength distribution per bin that Sobolev expansion opacity needs. OUTPUT: kappa(lambda, T). Compare with calibrated FAC/HULLAC/LANL/GRASP and validate on La II / Ce II measured levels. Scaling: f-occupation n and active-space size.
- **Quantum mechanism.** Second-quantized relativistic Hamiltonian simulation or QPE spectral sampling on thermal/typical states. Its cost scales with spectral resolution rather than the 1e6-1e8 lines per ion.
- **Classical twin.** Calibrated FAC; HULLAC; LANL CI; GRASP MCDHF/RCI; HFR/Cowan fits; CI+MBPT; kernel-polynomial/Lanczos spectral functions on large CI matrices (the fair twin).
- **Kill test first.** Decompose the existing code-to-code opacity spread into configuration completeness, calibration and correlation parts. If correlation is minor, a larger classical CI suffices (kill).

### S6 = C44. Dense electron-hole plasma dynamics through the exciton Mott crossover in 2D TMDs
- **Problem.** INPUT: m_e, m_h, valley/spin flavours, Rytova-Keldysh (r0, eps_env), and pump-created e/h distributions at 1e11-1e13 cm^-2 in a periodic cell with eta = 2 x (16-64) carriers. COMPUTE: real-time dynamics from a product-of-determinants initial state over the e-e-dominated window (up to ~100-200 fs). OUTPUT: time-resolved absorption/gain spectrum, exciton fraction, THz conductivity and n_M(t), compared with pump-probe data. Scaling: eta at fixed density, and density across the crossover.
- **Quantum mechanism.** First-quantized two-component 2D plane-wave simulation of the bare screened Coulomb Hamiltonian. No Gibbs preparation is needed. Readout by dipole/current autocorrelation or momentum occupations. The advantage is exponential over exact dynamics and an accuracy class over NEGF.
- **Classical twin.** Tuned G1-G2 NEGF (GW and T-matrix); SBE with correlation; excitonic Heisenberg equations; GW-BSE; equilibrium PIMC/DMC; small-cluster ED; time-dependent NQS.
- **Kill test first.** Two checks. Do GW-G1G2, T-matrix-G1G2 and SBE disagree on gain onset / n_M by more than experimental scatter at a fixed substrate? And does a cell with eta <= 128 hold the crossover (exciton radius vs cell size)?

## 4. Reserve (next best 6)
- **C02 WDM conductivity/Lorenz/G_ei.** The fallback if C01 fails L5. It shares C01's machinery, but novelty is C (a Sandia OSTI report exists) and the DC limit is costly.

## 5. Why chemistry and correlated-electron lattice models have no shortlist slot
Every chemistry-lens survivor (C32, C33, C34) states a polynomial advantage (QPE vs DMRG at fixed precision) and carries an active-space / dynamic-correlation floor. That is the FeMoco pattern (L6; 2601.04621), and classical ranking methods have repeatedly closed such gaps. Lattice-model candidates (C25-C31) fail on model-floor or decision-relevance grounds against the strongest classical frontier in the pool. C28 (impact ionization) is the runner-up if C44 dies.
