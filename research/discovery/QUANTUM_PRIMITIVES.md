# Quantum primitives considered in the discovery sprint

_2026-09-27. For each primitive, this file gives: the best known speedup and its model, what it needs (oracles, state preparation, promises), where it entered this sprint's candidates, and why it failed or what remains. The literature-phase catalogue is `research/literature/QUANTUM_PRIMITIVES.md`; keys refer to `research/literature/BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`._
