### QM-01: Warm-started walk / QSA / quantum replica-exchange Langevin sampler for the learned-energy structure posterior at >=60 aa (lead M1/M2 sampler core)

- **Merged from lane cards:** qalg QM-01 (QRELD warm start), qalg QM-03 (non-thermal QSA paths: residue-growth, likelihood-tempering), learned 'Information-annealed QSA from the (theta,tau) product prior', complexity 'QSA vs SMC stage-count lemma', classical 'Learned-energy posterior sampling (lead M1) twin portfolio', priorart (a) walk/QSA/QMS/RELD prior art, priorart (h) QSA-from-prior part
- **Consolidated verdict:** **KILLED**. Landscape-independent floor T*_Q ≥ 0.26 yr/sample (≥1 yr on the A80 target) even at 1 µs Toffolis. The pilot's 0 round trips is undiagnosed (ill-conditioning vs barrier). The T=1 soft readout ties the argmin (−0.10…+0.26 Å).

Q: can soft posterior-averaged structures (S33's 1.40x signal, obtained only as an exact enumerated sum) be sampled at 60-150 aa more cheaply than with the best classical sampler? Quantum: Szegedy/Lemieux Metropolis walk + (adaptive) QSA [A7,A8,A31]; operator-level QRELD / Witten-Laplacian QSVT [A44]. Replaces: only the mixing/rejuvenation kernel inside PT/SMC; energy, decoder, alignment and averaging stay classical. Mechanism: the walk has phase gap ~sqrt(delta), so reflections/Chebyshev polynomials T_k(P) with signed coefficients separate the stationary state in ~1/sqrt(delta) steps, where classical P^t needs ~1/delta. A sampling algorithm cannot realise signed mixtures without a sign problem. Warm start: the per-residue (theta,tau) head is a product state, prepared EXACTLY with <=255 rotations/residue, which removes the cold-start sqrt(N) penalty [A9]. Paths: temperature, likelihood exponent lambda, residue growth, |i-j| band. Lemma: BC >= (1+chi^2)^-1/2, so SMC's per-stage condition implies QSA's; stage counts are equal, and the whole gain is sum delta_k^-1/2 vs sum tau_k (no cloning: 1 sample/anneal; SMC resampling has no quantum analogue). Speedup: at most quadratic in the per-stage gap (walk steps). Oracle: coherent Delta-E/grad-E of the pair tables (QM-05); wall-clock break-even needs >=~1e10-1e16 classical steps per independent sample. Competitor: non-reversible PT/REST2 + HMC in (theta,tau), adaptive SMC/AIS, PERM growth, optimise-then-sample darting + MBAR basin masses, flow/BioEmu proposals with MH/IS correction, TT sampling, random-prior independence twin (DNR-06). Info vs comp: pi is entirely classical (ESM-2 -> esmprior); the quantum stage only mixes faster, and its value is capped by the readout under exact pi. S29-S33: whole-distribution consumption of a SPECIFIED posterior lies outside H-001 (KILLBOOK scope warning); DNR-05/06 twins and DNR-10's reopening burden (a measured mixing failure) apply in full. Must be run at a native-free calibrated T* >> 1: at T=1 solver-best states differ by 50-230 nats and sampling collapses to restart-saturated search. Kill: C_class(150) <= 1e9 evaluations to certified agreement (4 method x seed runs agree on posterior mean within 0.1 A and on >=1%-mass basins within 0.05, planted-well control detected) with C_class(L) < B(L) to 300 aa; OR the SAMPLED soft readout fails to beat the A80 argmin by >=1.0x MDE on long40; OR a first-order path signature (bimodal histograms, overlaps ~e^-cL) that breaks QSA's premise too. Test: long40 + mid30 (+ tuning126 for the fit); NRPT (8-16 chains, HMC) + adaptive SMC (256-1000 particles) along lambda/growth paths from the head prior + darting twin, 4 seeds, ~1e7 evals/target; record tau_int, round trips, Lambda(L), per-stage BC/chi^2/ESS, AIS log Z, soft-readout MDE; fit against L. ~1 day on 8 cores. This campaign also feeds QM-02/03/07/11/12/15. Track: B fault-tolerant (C theory). Prior: low (~5%).

### QM-02: Hide-and-seek narrow wells: provable continuous-Gibbs separation vs white-box discovery, calibration and the DMDGP ceiling

- **Merged from lane cards:** qalg QM-02 (PT + quantum narrow-well discovery), complexity 'Hide-and-seek on the backbone torus (DMDGP ceiling)', learned 'Composite-likelihood overconfidence as source of hide-and-seek wells', classical 'Continuous quantum Langevin (lead M2) vs white-box mode discovery'
- **Consolidated verdict:** **KILLED**. The pair tables are white-box. Expected-distance DG reached the deepest known basin in 61/64 runs at 1–5% of restart/PT cost. Prior sublevel mass ≤ e^−KL, with KL = 113–960 nats.

Q: does the learned-energy posterior at T* contain narrow, deep basins that carry mass (persistence / hide-and-seek) yet are almost never reached by prior-initialised descents or PT? Quantum: Olivucci et al. [A45] continuous Gibbs sampler, Omega(alpha) classical vs O~(sqrt(alpha)) quantum queries, alpha = e^{beta*Delta}; minimal form is fixed-point AA of the product head prior onto a native-free sublevel set {E < E_thr}. Replaces: rare-mode discovery only; masses, polishing and averaging stay classical. Mechanism: amplitudes add under the Grover iterate (k*sqrt(p)) while classical trials add probabilities. The lower bound covers value/gradient/any-derivative QUERIES in the black-box model. Caveats: (i) classical restarts target the basin of attraction p_att >> the sublevel-set volume p, so quantum wins only if 1/sqrt(p) < 1/p_att; (ii) the energy is WHITE-BOX, a sum of 1-D pair tables. Case A (unimodal tables, rigid sharp-pair graph): DG/EDM locates wells from table minima in poly(L), which dequantises the separation. Case B (k-modal tables, register ambiguity): locating wells is a DMDGP instance (NP-hard) and quantum backtracking is again quadratic in the tree. Claimed ceiling: max(quadratic in alpha, quadratic in T_BP). Likely origin: E_pair is an unadjusted pairwise composite likelihood (Varin-Reid-Firth), so clusters of confidently wrong bins (1,168 ESM false-positive contacts, 57% within +-2 register) create narrow, overconfident, artefactual wells that native-free recalibration can delete. Speedup: quadratic in alpha (query). Oracle: threshold/gradient oracle (QM-05); pays only if p_att < ~1e-10 per mass-carrying basin. Competitor: table-cluster enumeration + cluster-seeded L-BFGS, darting/mode-jumping MH, lambda-tempering of long-range pair terms, nested sampling, NRPT. Info vs comp: wells are properties of the model's calibration; if calibration removes them there was never a computational problem. S29-S33: E800b restart saturation (7.08 -> 4.35 A by 32-64 restarts, floor 4.21 A) is direct evidence of case A at 44-60 aa; M9/DNR-07 bound restart gains at <=0.14 A. Open only at >=100 aa. Kill: min p_hit >= 1e-4 for every >=1%-mass basin at 60-150 aa; OR >=90% of high-mass narrow modes are seeded by top-k table clusters; OR at calibrated T* narrow modes hold <5% mass or are not >=3x narrower (funnel). Test: calibrate T* on held-out prots/ by posterior-predictive coverage (never long40 RMSD); on 12 long40 targets run 1,000 prior-initialised descents + 24-replica PT; cluster; record basin mass, Hessian log-volume, p_hit, Woodard persistence, DG coverage of PT modes, planted-well control; repeat at T=1. ~1 day. Track: C theory/query, then B. Prior: low (3-5%).

### QM-03: Gates on what any faster sampler can buy: readout-visible slow modes (delta_f, H(B)), walk-vs-lift ratio sqrt(delta)/Phi, and learned-transport preconditioning

- **Merged from lane cards:** complexity 'Readout-specific mixing gate (delta_f, H(B))', complexity 'Quantum walk vs every classical lift: ratio Theta(sqrt(delta)/Phi)', learned 'Learned transport maps shrink what quantum Langevin accelerates (C_PI in latent coordinates)'
- **Consolidated verdict:** **KILLED** (kept as a classical instrument; fold into T2). Basin decomposition does not depend on Φ, so the claim that "the full quadratic survives in the metastable regime" is measured against a straw man. Two gate formulas are wrong as written.

Q: after the strongest classical adversaries are applied, how much of QM-01's quadratic gain is left, and does it touch the built chain? Quantum: walk-based mean estimation / QRELD (as QM-01). Mechanism, three proven or derivable gates (I(prog)): (1) Readout gate: expand the soft-readout functional f = sum c_k phi_k. The classical cost is O~(1/delta_f + tau_f sigma^2/eps^2), with delta_f the gap of the slow modes f actually overlaps, while walk estimation pays the GLOBAL gap. If delta_f >> delta (e.g. a mirror-swap slow mode with identical aligned mean), classical wins regardless. The accuracy value of any sampler is bounded by H(B) = RMSD(readout at classical budget B) - RMSD(readout under exact pi). (2) Lift gate: a lift cannot raise conductance, so t_mix(lift) >= 1/(4 Phi), while the walk costs ~1/sqrt(delta); by Cheeger, T_lift/T_QW = Omega(sqrt(delta)/Phi). Diffusive regime (delta ~ Phi^2): lifts/NRPT match the walk. Metastable regime (delta ~ Phi): the full quadratic survives against every lift of that chain. (3) Transport gate: compare sqrt(C_PI(x)) * C_grad^Q against C_PI(z) * C_grad^C in NeuTra/flow latents or prior-quantile coordinates u = F_q(theta,tau). If C_PI(z) <= sqrt(C_PI(x)), quantum loses before overhead. Only barriers flows cannot remove survive (plausibly QM-02's narrow wells). Speedup: none of its own; bounds QM-01/12/15. Oracle: none (classical measurements). Competitor: NRPT, lifted chains, NeuTra-HMC, u-space HMC, MSM estimates. Info vs comp: H(infinity) is set by esmprior information; only H(B) - H(infinity) is computational headroom. S29-S33: sharpens DNR-10's reopening condition from 'mixing failure' to 'mixing failure visible to the readout functional'. Kill (quantum relevance): soft readout at <=1e7 evals within 0.3x MDE of a 1e9 / exact-register reference; OR delta_f/delta >= 100; OR delta/Phi^2 <= 10 at all L in 44-120; OR latent tau_int <= sqrt(raw tau_int). Test: 10 mid30 + 10 long40 targets, PT at 1e5-1e8 evals for the H(B) curve, TICA+MSM (200-500 states) for delta, Phi (PCCA+) and projections onto the 5 slowest modes; HMC raw vs u-space vs small-IAF NeuTra, 25->60 aa. 10-20 CPU-hours, reusing the QM-01 runs. Track: C theory/query. Prior: low for a surviving gain; high decision value.

### QM-04: Cooperative (first-order-like) folding regime: quadratic speedup of a stretched-exponential tempering cost with explicit break-even length L*

- **Merged from lane cards:** complexity 'Cooperative (first-order-like) regime ... break-even L*' (plus torpid-mixing arguments cited in qalg QM-03/QM-12)
- **Consolidated verdict:** **KILLED**. T*_Q does not depend on ΔF(L). Order-parameter and entropy-dampened biasing is polynomial (Bhatnagar–Randall, the card's own source). Physical barriers are ≲ 23–35 kT, below 2 ln R.

Q: if the learned energy reproduces two-state cooperative folding at 100-300 aa, does tempering become torpid, and at what length would quantised tempering pay after FT overhead? Quantum: walk/QSA on a simulated-tempering or replica-exchange chain [A8,A31]; QRELD [A44]. Replaces: parallel or simulated tempering. Mechanism: at a first-order transition tempering gaps are <= e^{-Theta(DeltaF)} with an interfacial barrier DeltaF ~ sigma L^{2/3} (rigorous for mean-field Potts [E40]; lattice multicanonical e^{DeltaF}, Neuhaus-Hager; the protein capillarity coefficient is UNVERIFIED). A walk on the same chain costs e^{DeltaF/2}. The gain is only a square root, but the absolute saving e^{DeltaF/2} is exponential in L^{2/3}, the only asymptotic route around 'quadratic does not pay on FT hardware'. Break-even (I(prog)): R e^{DeltaF/2} < e^{DeltaF}, i.e. DeltaF > 2 ln R, so L* = (2 ln R/sigma)^{3/2}, about 180-310 aa for sigma = 1 kT and R = 1e7-1e10, where both sides still cost ~R^2 steps. Speedup: walk steps, sqrt of e^{Theta(L^{2/3})}. Oracle: coherent E + tempering-index register; R ~1e7-1e10 (QM-05). Competitor: umbrella/metadynamics/2-D Wang-Landau along a NATIVE-FREE order parameter (learned-Q from esmprior argmax contacts, plus energy), REST2, population annealing. Energy-only multicanonical does not remove interfacial barriers, so the adversary needs a good coordinate. Info vs comp: cooperativity is a model property; crossing is computation. S29-S33: never beyond 60 aa, tempering never measured; old equal-tuning twins were cheap, and here the twin is torpid by assumption and must be replaced by the reaction-coordinate adversary. Kill: C_v peak per residue flat and energy histograms unimodal at 45-120 aa; OR learned-Q umbrella / 2-D WL round trips polynomial (degree <= 3); OR Lee-Kosterlitz DeltaF extrapolates below 2 ln R (~32-46 kT) for L <= 300. Test: 24-32-temperature PT at L = 30/45/60 (instruments) and 80/100/120 (native-free sequences, flagged as OOD for esmprior's <=72-aa training crops), 3 per length; histograms, Lee-Kosterlitz barrier, C_max/L, round trips; learned-Q umbrella at two lengths. ~1 day. Track: B fault-tolerant. Prior: low (smooth pair-distance energies are unlikely to be strongly cooperative).

S29-S33: never costed physical runtime; fills gaps G-2/G-6 and sharpens H-008; gates QM-01/02/04/12/13/18. ### QM-06: Screening no-gos: consumption-factoring theorem with per-primitive quadratic ceilings (generalised H-001, GHV/LWWZ exception) and cheap-gradient dequantisation of quantum gradient estimation

Q: when can a quantum stage acting on a classical learned energy be load-bearing by more than a quadratic factor? (3) Composition lemma (I(prog)): sequential or nested compositions of <= quadratic primitives stay <= quadratic in total classical cost. (4) Explicit exception: the pure value-query separations of GHV -> LWWZ (poly vs exp(n^{1/5-o(1)})) mean this is NOT 'everything is quadratic'; any super-quadratic protein claim must exhibit GHV-type oracle structure (QM-09) or planted algebraic structure (QM-17). (5) Cheap gradients: reverse-mode AD gives exact grad E in <= c*S operations (Baur-Strassen; UNVERIFIED here), so claimed d-fold Jordan/GAW gains shrink by d/c (d ~ 100-300) against white-box energies. Only separations whose classical lower bound already allows derivative queries survive (Olivucci does; Zhang-Li prove none for stationary points; LWWZ must be re-checked against derivative queries). Speedup: none (L6 ceiling). Info vs comp: data processing; the quantum stage only changes the cost of Prim(E). S29-S33: generalises R1-R4, R9, K-001/002/004/011 and DNR-02 from the deployed CVaR family to walks, QAE and rejection sampling (T1/H-009). Track: C theory/query. Prior: this is a no-go; high probability it holds as scoped.
