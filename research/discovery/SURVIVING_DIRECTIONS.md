**No candidate survives as a quantum-advantage claim.** The directions below are the reopen conditions that the kills left open, ranked by evidence × decisiveness of a cheap test × prize.

- **E (evidence for the premise):** 1 = none or contrary; 2 = pilot or indirect; 3 = replicated.
- **D (decisiveness):** 1 = paper work, or an uncertain test; 2 = a test of ≤ 1 day that decides part of the question; 3 = a test of ≤ 1 day that decides it fully.
- **P (prize to the mission):** 1 = closes a question or yields classical science only; 2 = a conditional polynomial or sample-complexity gain; 3 = a super-quadratic or exponential separation in principle.
