Titles lose trailing descriptive tags added by the notes (e.g. "(MBAR)", "(Jiuzhang)"); each removed tag is recorded in Notes as "tag used in the note". No metadata was invented; where a field was not recorded it is left out (e.g. no year for #17, #18, #180).
