## 1. Quantum-component methodology

1. **Equal tuning effort, not just equal budget.** "SA TUNING (H_hybrid E1001): equal-effort tuned SA beats CVaR-VQE on energy (3.394 vs 3.457) on the A41 register; P_prior's 41/45 VQE win was an SA-tuning artefact. LESSON: every VQE-vs-SA claim needs equal tuning effort, not just equal evaluation budget." (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md`). QUANTUM_CONTRASTS carries an "Equal tuning?" column for this reason ("That is the E1001 lesson: budget-matched but untuned SA flattered VQE on energy") (`s33/VERIFICATION/final/QUANTUM_CONTRASTS.md`).
2. **Beat SA/greedy at equal n_evals, not random.** "any S33 quantum claim must beat SA/greedy at equal n_evals, not random" (`s33/LEDGER.md` E003). "VQE designs must (i) keep the parameter count small / structured, (ii) warm-start from classical priors, (iii) exploit exact gradients where n<=20, (iv) be judged against SA/greedy at equal n_evals." (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md` ENGINE FACTS).
3. **Early budget is random sampling.** "At equal budget the early CVaR-VQE *is* random sampling. ... **Report `trace` (best vs n_evals) and `evaluated_tail` next to the classical controls, never the in-run CVaR alone.**" (`s33/ARCHITECTURES/QENGINE.md` §7.1).
4. **Re-score returned ensembles.** "In-run CVaR on a noisy or stochastic energy is selection-biased low for every method (negative control 8). Always re-score the returned ensemble." (`QENGINE.md` §7.5).
5. **Condition C before any build.** "A structural register moves the problem from 'the circuit has nothing to compute' to 'the circuit computes a hard problem whose energy must recognise nativeness' — condition C must be pre-checked before any build." (`s33/MAP_QUANTUM.md` §0.3). "the register change is necessary, not sufficient" (§3).
6. **Solver-equivalence limits where a quantum stage can matter.** "'the quantum stage changes the answer' can only come from (i) optimisation quality ... or (ii) a readout that uses the whole output distribution" (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md`).
7. **Diversity, not concentration, helps readouts.** "Diversity-preserving averages of chain-like objects help. Concentration (CVaR, argmin of a weak energy) and averages of contracted objects hurt." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §14). "Broad (random-prior) sampling avoids them [topology traps], and concentrated optimisation (CVaR, cold SA) falls into them." (§26.9).
8. **Register energies must transmit.** "The orderings of discrete register energies do not survive relaxation" (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §7); "The only energies whose low states have lower RMSD *in the operating band* are **continuous likelihoods of predicted pair geometry, evaluated on single built structures**." (§7).
9. **Re-open quantum search only with a pre-registered search gap.** "Quantum search should be re-opened only where a pre-registered search gap exists." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §1.7); S34 re-test design: register around the decoder's residual uncertainty (A82), twins "equal-*tuning* SA, random-prior sampling and a product ansatz", primary contrast "VQE − random-prior tail on the relaxed chain", kill "if condition C restricted to the neighbourhood of the decoder's answer fails, or if random ≤ VQE" (§28.4). "A better energy is the precondition for any register search to have headroom." (§29.5).
10. **Simulation engineering.** "Multithreaded BLAS slows MPS; run 1 BLAS thread per process and parallelise across processes." (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md`); "write our own light MPS ... do NOT pip-install quimb" (`s33/MAP_QUANTUM.md` §0.4); repo MPS usable only to χ = 16 (§0.5).

## 2. Structure-prediction / architecture lessons

11. **Information switches on with length; the optimal architecture changes with length.** "INFORMATION SWITCHES ON WITH LENGTH"; "The optimal architecture CHANGES WITH LENGTH (M_mid): distogram-prior averaging (production) wins at 13 aa; ESM-loglik narrow selection and ESM-geometry decoding win at 33 and 55 aa" (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md` Phase 2).
12. **Decode, don't select, at length.** "DECODING, NOT SELECTING, is the long-chain lever" (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md`). "The bottleneck is information ... It is not search and not decoding." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §1.7).
13. **The prior is the steep lever.** "Prior quality moved every long-chain decoder"; "Nothing else in S33 has a demonstrated positive slope." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §29.1).
14. **Distance accuracy ≠ selection value.** "Distance accuracy and selection value are decoupled in this regime." (`s33/MAP_LEARNED.md` §0.3); "the useful direction is target-specific (towards the native), not 'lower MAE'" (§6.1); "Better MAE does not mean better selection at peptide length." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §12).
15. **Decode through CA virtual angles.** "at length DECODE VIA CA VIRTUAL ANGLES (θ,τ) or project, never by raw φ/ψ concatenation." (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md` Phase 1; `s33/MAP_QUANTUM.md` §5.1).
16. **Templates must be gated, not relaxed.** "Templates are better than any modelled structure, and relaxing them into an energy with a 2–3 Å information error destroys them. Hence the gate." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §13.6). Gate identity: "Gating cannot inflate significance." (§25.17).
17. **Averaging contracts; energies on averages mislead.** Averaging lemma: "a distance-likelihood energy ranks an ensemble mean *below* its members exactly when RMSD ranks it *above* them" (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §25.9). Projection charges contracted inputs (+0.16–0.20 Å vs +0.03–0.06 for decoded clouds) (§10, §15).
18. **Every long-length mechanism transferred to 9–16 aa was harmful.** (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §11.3). At 9–16 aa "no native-free channel carries nonlocal pair information" (§1.1).
19. **Local fragment information cannot fold a 55-mer.** Lever-arm law: "Local fragment information cannot fold a 55-mer; a global term must pin the lever arms." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §25.8).

## 3. Measurement, statistics and leakage discipline

20. **Same-job, same-bits pairing.** "ALWAYS same-bits, same-job baselines." (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md` Phase 1). "Every S33 arm must therefore be compared against a production arm **projected in the same job from the same stored cloud bits**" (`s33/MAP_PIPELINE.md` §7.1). "only same-job, same-bits chain pairs are exact. Every cross-job contrast in this report is either licensed by bit-identical sentinels or flagged." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §22).
21. **S32's methodology lesson carried forward.** S32's 11 defects "were all 'a number re-used across a boundary'"; S33 response: same-job sentinels, bit-identity licensing, per-lane pre-registration, sprint-wide arm census with best-of-k corrections; "The census flagged stale STATE figures ... and corrected them" (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §3).
22. **Best-of-k correction of headline claims.** Family = all candidate arms of the instrument; five corrections (Bonferroni, best_of_k_accounted, winner's curse, split-half, Westfall-Young) (`s33/VERIFICATION/final/CLAIM_CENSUS.md` §2).
23. **Cross-fold homologues can leak through out-of-fold models.** "the map-learned LFO long40 distogram is out-of-fold but long40's folds contain CROSS-FOLD HOMOLOGUES (13 pairs NW > 0.40)" (`s33/SCIENTIFIC_MEMORY.md` LEAKAGE); esmprior_v1's R3 rule (gapless-30 identity ≥ 0.4 to ANY long40 target) removed them (§21 of REPORT).
24. **ESM feature hazard.** "any call of `core.predict.features`, `pn_*_features`, `data.esm_raw/esm_contacts` on a long40 or other new sequence must be fenced" (`s33/MAP_LEARNED.md` §1.3).
25. **Leakage safety by convention.** "`rr`, `nat_ca` stored in the **same npz** as the deployable arrays ... leakage safety of any instrument-path arm is by convention, not by construction" (`s33/MAP_PIPELINE.md` §4 D11).
26. **Projection is chaotic.** "A 1e-14 Å change in the cloud moves individual chains by up to 1.78 Å, and moves the mean by 0.004 Å." (`s33/REPORT_S33.md` §2); "λ = 0 vs λ = 0.3 never changes a verdict" (§15).
27. **NMR reference floor.** ~+0.16 Å of tuning126 endpoint is reference noise "that no architecture can recover" (`s33/FALSIFICATION_CATALOGUE.md` §5.3).
